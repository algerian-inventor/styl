Place all your custom website images (like .jpg, .png, .webp, .svg) inside this folder.

How to name them:
Keep names simple, lowercase, and use dashes instead of spaces. 
Example names:
- hero-banner.jpg
- robotics-club.png
- event-1.jpg

How to use them in your HTML/React code:
<img src="/images/your-file-name.jpg" alt="..." className="..." />
(Do not include the word "public" in the code path).
